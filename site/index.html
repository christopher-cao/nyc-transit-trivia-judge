How to play
  • Pick subway/train lines in order to build the fastest route between the two landmarks.
  • You have up to 5 guesses. Each wrong guess reveals how close you were.
  • Walk time to/from stations is capped at 30 minutes — no crossing water on foot.
  • Times are based on real NYC schedules for the puzzle's day and time.
  • Give up anytime to see the optimal route.
